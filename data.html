Home / Data

Data

Eight continuous data streams from the COSZO and RCA sensor suite, covering ground motion, pressure, acoustic, and water-velocity measurements. All data are openly available.

All Products

The full data catalog.

Each data product is served as a continuous real-time stream and as archived records, accessible through the OOI data portal.

Broadband Seismometer & Strong Motion

Three-component broadband ground velocity together with high-dynamic-range strong-motion acceleration.

Motion · Seismometer

Short-Period Seismometer

High-frequency seismometer data for local microseismicity and P-wave detection.

Motion · Short-period

Differential Pressure Gauge

Short-period seafloor pressure variations capturing tsunami signals and hydroacoustic waves.

Pressure · Differential

Hydrophone

Hydrophone data in the infrasound band for T-phase and hydroacoustic monitoring.

Acoustic · Hydrophone

Absolute Pressure Gauge

Total pressure at the seafloor for tsunami detection and seafloor deformation monitoring.

Pressure · Absolute

GSSM — Calibrated Pressure & Acceleration

Combined calibrated pressure and strong-motion records from geodetic seafloor stations.

Composite · Calibrated

CSCPR — Calibrated Pressure

Cabled self-calibrating pressure recorder data corrected for instrument drift.

Pressure · Drift-corrected

Current Meter

Current meter and ADCP measurements of sea-water velocity through the water column.

Water · Current meter
COSZO

The Cascadia Offshore Subduction Zone Observatory is an NSF Mid-scale Research Infrastructure project adding geophysical instrumentation to the OOI Regional Cabled Array off the coast of Oregon to understand the science of subduction-zone hazard.
